## v4.2 assembly

Use the [v4.2 delivery notes](production/revision_v4_2/V4_2_DELIVERY_NOTES.md) and
[cue timings](production/revision_v4_2/v4_2_delivery.json) to assemble the current cut.

1. Start with the [narrated opening](production/revision_v4_2/01_opening_v4_1.mp4) (34.00 seconds).
2. Add the owner-edited screen recording using the [ten demo stems](production/revision_v4_2/audio/demo).
3. Add the [narrated explainer](production/revision_v4_2/03_under_the_hood_v4.mp4) (121.42 seconds).
   Insert the Galileo recording at 84.00 seconds in its 418-frame slot and apply the
   [Galileo lower third](production/revision_v4_2/galileo_lower_third.png).

The [silent explainer](production/revision_v4_2/03_under_the_hood_v4_silent.mp4) and
[separate narration](production/revision_v4_2/03_under_the_hood_v4_narration.wav) support local editing.

The [v4.1 handoff](production/revision_v4_1/V4_1_DELIVERY_NOTES.md) is available for reference.

The [v4 handoff](production/revision_v4/V4_DELIVERY_NOTES.md) is available for reference.

## v3.2 assembly

Use the [v3.2 assembly instructions](production/revision_v3_2_assembly/README.md)
for the previous cut. The 70-second explainer uses the approved 12-second
architectural reveal and fixed labels for the handoffs.

| Order | Material | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Narrated opening](production/revision_v3_2_assembly/media/01_opening.mp4) | 14 seconds |
| 2 | Your re-edited recording with [demo narration](production/revision_v3_2_assembly/audio/02_demo_narration.wav) | 101.283 seconds at the aligned reference duration |
| 3 | [Narrated v3.2 explainer](production/revision_v3_2_assembly/media/03_under_the_hood_v3_2.mp4) | 70 seconds |

Align the recording against the [demo cue sheet](production/revision_v3_2_assembly/edit/demo_cues_actual.csv)
and supplied stems, then use the [assembly helper](production/revision_v3_2_assembly/assemble_v3_2.py)
to fit its full frame into the 720p, 24 fps film. Final alignment and export
require your re-edited recording.

The [v3.1 assembly](production/revision_v3_1_assembly/README.md) and
[connected v3 production sources](production/revision_v3_connected/README.md)
are available for reference.
