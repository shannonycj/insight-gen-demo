Now, let's see how we make this work in the Amex business setting.

General-purpose agents bring reasoning and tools. Our differentiator is the enterprise system around them: business-owned knowledge, reusable expert methods, controlled data access, and evidence-driven improvement.

Business teams curate definitions, policies, and context in Enterprise Knowledge Foundation. Their analytical expertise becomes reusable primitives and skills through Skill Foundry.

The C-sixty definition grounds comparison, segmentation, and testing; those primitives compose into a governed skill, with its source links retained.

Teams use InsightGen to apply that knowledge and skill, connect to permitted data, and return findings with evidence.

As the agent runs, the Agent Observability Framework captures its execution trace and quality signals.

Harness Intelligence uses the evidence to diagnose, test, and validate a change. The improved skill returns to Skill Foundry and the next agent run.
