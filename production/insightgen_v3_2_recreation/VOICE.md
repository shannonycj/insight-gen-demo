# Voice direction and production settings

The [approved voice sample](references/approved_voice.mp3) is the casting reference. Use a warm, approachable female American-English technology demonstrator speaking to senior financial-services executives. Aim for confident energy, clear diction and natural conversational momentum. Give key business-value phrases gentle emphasis. Keep the pitch and cadence measured enough to sound professional. Avoid a sharp or piercing tone, low energy, exaggerated announcer delivery, long dramatic pauses and promotional inflection.

| Setting | Approved v3.2 value |

| Voice | `English_ConfidentWoman` |

| Language | English with American delivery |

Use the existing approved masters where available. For fresh synthesis, use the same approved settings across the opening, nine demo paragraphs and eight explainer cues. For another provider, choose a comparable voice by listening to a short sample, then use one consistent voice and pace throughout. Speed scales vary by provider. Match the approved sample by ear. Treat the sample as a listening reference for style and pacing. Choose a licensed system voice. The sample is approved for listening and casting.

Preserve the approved speech tempo, pitch and full words across segments. Review pronunciation, energy, intelligibility, clipping and joins in the combined mix. Use narration as the soundtrack in this recreation.
